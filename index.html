The Montessori Approach

Why Montessori Works for Early Literacy

The Montessori method builds literacy skills through a carefully sequenced, sensory-rich approach that respects each child's natural development.

Phonological Awareness

Children learn to identify and manipulate sounds within words through games and activities that make learning enjoyable.

Sandpaper Letters

Tactile learning reinforces letter recognition by engaging multiple senses—visual, auditory, and kinesthetic.

Movable Alphabet

Children compose words physically before writing them, developing confidence in spelling and word construction.

Child-Led Learning

Following the child's interests and readiness leads to enthusiastic learners who develop a lifelong love of reading.
